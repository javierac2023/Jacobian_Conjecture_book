Proposition 1.7.8.

If \((P,Q)\) is a counterexample to the plane Jacobian conjecture, then there exist coprime \(m,n \in \mathbb{N}\) such that, for all \((\rho,\sigma) \in [(1,-1),(0,1)]\text{,}\) we have
🔗
  1. \(v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = \frac{n}{m} v_{\rho\sigma}(P)\text{,}\)
    🔗
  2. \(v_{\rho\sigma}(P) > 0\) and \(v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) > 0\text{,}\)
    🔗
  3. \([\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(Q)] = 0\text{,}\)
    🔗
  4. \(\operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = \frac{n}{m} \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(P)\) and \(\operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = \frac{n}{m} \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(P)\text{,}\)
    🔗
  5. \(\operatorname{Dir}(P) \cap [(1,-1),(0,1)] = \operatorname{Dir}(Q) \cap [(1,-1),(0,1)]\text{.}\)
    🔗
