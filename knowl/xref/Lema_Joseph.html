Lemma 1.5.3.

Let \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\) and let \((P,Q)\) be a Jacobian pair. If \(v_{\rho\sigma}(P) \ne 0\text{,}\) then there exists \(G_0 \in K[P,Q]\) such that
\begin{equation*} [\ell_{\rho\sigma}(G_0), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(P)] \ne 0 \quad\text{and}\quad [[\ell_{\rho\sigma}(G_0), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(P)], \ell_{\rho\sigma}(P)] = 0. \end{equation*}
Moreover, if we define recursively \(G_i := [G_{i-1}, P]\text{,}\) then \([\ell_{\rho\sigma}(G_i), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(P)] = 0\text{,}\) for \(i \ge 1\text{.}\)
