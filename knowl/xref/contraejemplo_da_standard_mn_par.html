Proposition 1.7.19.

For each counterexample \((P,Q)\) such that \(v_{11}(P)\) and \(v_{11}(Q)\) do not divide each other, there exists an automorphism \(\varphi\) of \(K[x,y]\) such that \((\varphi(P),\varphi(Q))\) is a subrectangular counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture, which is a standard \((m,n)\)-pair, for some \(m,n \in \mathbb{N}\text{.}\) Moreover
\begin{equation} \frac{v_{11}(\varphi(P))}{v_{11}(\varphi(Q))} = \frac{v_{11}(P)}{v_{11}(Q)} \quad\text{and}\quad v_{11}(\varphi(P)) \le v_{11}(P).\tag{1.7.6} \end{equation}
