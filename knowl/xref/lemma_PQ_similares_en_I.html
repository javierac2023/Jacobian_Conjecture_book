Lemma 1.7.7.

Assume that the Jacobian conjecture is false, let \((P,Q)\) be a counterexample to it and let \((\rho,\sigma) \in [(1,-1),(0,1)] \subseteq \mathfrak{V}\text{.}\) Assume that there exist coprime \(m,n \in \mathbb{N}\text{,}\) such that
\begin{equation} \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = \frac{n}{m} \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(P).\tag{1.7.2} \end{equation}
Then
🔗
  1. \(v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = \frac{n}{m} v_{\rho\sigma}(P)\text{,}\)
    🔗
  2. \(v_{\rho\sigma}(P), v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) > 0\text{,}\)
    🔗
  3. \([\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(Q)] = 0\text{,}\)
    🔗
  4. \((\rho,\sigma) \in \operatorname{Dir}(P)\) if and only if \((\rho,\sigma) \in \operatorname{Dir}(Q)\text{,}\)
    🔗
  5. \(\operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = \frac{n}{m} \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(P)\text{.}\)
    🔗
