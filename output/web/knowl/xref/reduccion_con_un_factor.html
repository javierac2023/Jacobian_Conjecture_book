Proposition 1.7.15.

Assume the Jacobian conjecture is false and let \((P,Q)\) be a counterexample to it. Assume that \(v_{11}(P)\) and \(v_{11}(Q)\) do not divide each other and that we are in one of the cases 1b), 1c) or 2a) of Remark 1.7.12. Then there exists an automorphism \(\varphi\) of \(K[x,y]\) such that
\begin{equation} \frac{v_{11}(\varphi(P))}{v_{11}(\varphi(Q))} = \frac{v_{11}(P)}{v_{11}(Q)} \quad\text{and}\quad v_{11}(\varphi(P)) < v_{11}(P).\tag{1.7.3} \end{equation}
Consequently, \(\gcd(\deg(\varphi(P)), \deg(\varphi(Q))) < \gcd(\deg(P), \deg(Q))\text{.}\)
