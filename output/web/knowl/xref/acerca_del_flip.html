Remark 1.4.16.

Let \(P \in K[x,y]\text{,}\) \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\) and let \(\tau := \varphi_{y,x}\) be the automorphism of \(K[x,y]\) that interchanges \(x\) and \(y\text{.}\) A direct computation shows that \(\ell_{\rho\sigma}(\tau(P)) = \tau(\ell_{\sigma\rho}(P))\text{.}\) From this and equalities (1.4.2) it follows that \(\operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(\tau(P)) = \overline{\tau}(\operatorname{en}_{\sigma\rho}(P))\) and \(\operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(\tau(P)) = \overline{\tau}(\operatorname{stn}_{\sigma\rho}(P))\text{,}\) where \(\overline{\tau}\) denotes the flip of \(\mathbb{R}^2\text{.}\)
