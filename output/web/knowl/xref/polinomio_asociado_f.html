Remark 1.4.18.

Let \(P \in K[x,y]\setminus\{0\}\) and \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\text{.}\) Set \(z := x^{-\sigma} y^{\rho} \in K[x,y,x^{-1},y^{-1}]\) and write
\begin{equation*} \ell_{\rho\sigma}(P) = x^r y^s p(z), \end{equation*}
where \(p := \sum_{i=0}^{\gamma} a_i z^i \in K[z]\) with \(a_0 \ne 0\) and \(a_{\gamma} \ne 0\text{.}\) Since \(v_{\sigma,-\rho}(z) = -\rho^2 - \sigma^2 < 0\text{,}\) by (1.4.2) we have
\begin{equation} \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(P) = (r,s) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(P) = (r - \gamma\sigma,\; s + \gamma\rho).\tag{1.4.3} \end{equation}
