Proposition 1.5.5.

Let \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}_{>0}\) and let \(R,F \in K[x,y]\setminus\{0\}\) be \((\rho,\sigma)\)-homogeneous elements. Assume that \(v_{\rho\sigma}(R) \ge 0\) and that there exists \(k \ge 1\) such that
\begin{equation} [R,F] = R^k.\tag{1.5.8} \end{equation}
Define \(z := x^{-\sigma} y^{\rho} \in K[x,y,x^{-1},y^{-1}]\text{,}\) and write
\begin{equation} R = x^i y^j r(z) \quad\text{and}\quad F = x^u y^v f(z), \quad \text{with } r(z), f(z) \in K[z],\; f(0) \ne 0,\; r(0) \ne 0.\tag{1.5.9} \end{equation}
Then
🔗
  1. \(v_{\rho\sigma}(F) = (k-1) v_{\rho\sigma}(R) + \rho + \sigma\text{.}\)
    🔗
  2. Every irreducible factor of \(r(z)\) divides \(f(z)\text{.}\)
    🔗
  3. If \(f(z)\) is a constant (i.e., \(F\) is a monomial), then \(r(z)\) is also a constant (i.e., \(R\) is a monomial).
    🔗
  4. If \(\deg(f(z)) = 1\text{,}\) then \(r(z) \sim (z - \lambda)^{\ell}\text{,}\) for some \(\lambda \in K^{\times}\) and \(\ell \in \mathbb{N}_0\text{.}\)
    🔗
  5. \begin{equation} R^k = x^{i+u-1} y^{j+v-1} \left( v_{\rho\sigma}(R) z r(z) f'(z) - v_{\rho\sigma}(F) z f(z) r'(z) + (iv - ju) r(z) f(z) \right).\tag{1.5.10} \end{equation}
    🔗
