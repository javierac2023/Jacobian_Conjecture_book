Remark 1.4.11.

Let \(P,Q \in K[x,y]\setminus\{0\}\) and let \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\text{.}\) We have:
🔗
  1. If \(P\) and \(Q\) are \((\rho,\sigma)\)-homogeneous, then \([P,Q]\) is also. Moreover, if \([P,Q] \ne 0\text{,}\) then
    \begin{equation*} v_{\rho\sigma}([P,Q]) = v_{\rho\sigma}(P) + v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) - (\rho+\sigma). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(P = \sum_i P_i\) and \(Q = \sum_j Q_j\) are the \((\rho,\sigma)\)-homogeneous decompositions of \(P\) and \(Q\text{,}\) then the \((\rho,\sigma)\)-homogeneous decomposition \([P,Q] = \sum_k [P,Q]_k\) is given by
    \begin{equation} [P,Q]_k = \sum_{i+j = k + \rho + \sigma} [P_i, Q_j].\tag{1.4.1} \end{equation}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. If \([P,Q] = 0\text{,}\) then \([\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(Q)] = 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
