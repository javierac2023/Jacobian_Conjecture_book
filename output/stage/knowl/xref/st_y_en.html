Remark 1.4.12.

The identities
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(PQ) = \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(P) + \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(PQ) = \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(P) + \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) \end{equation*}
hold for each \(P,Q \in K[x,y]\setminus\{0\}\) and \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\text{.}\) Moreover, we have
\begin{equation} \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(P) = \operatorname{Supp}(\ell_{\sigma,-\rho}(\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P))) \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(P) = \operatorname{Supp}(\ell_{-\sigma,\rho}(\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P))),\tag{1.4.2} \end{equation}
as is illustrated in Figure 1.4.13.
