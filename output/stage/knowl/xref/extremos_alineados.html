Proposition 1.4.20.

Let \(P,Q \in K[x,y]\setminus \{0\}\) and \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\text{.}\) If \([\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(Q)] = 0\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(P) \times \operatorname{stn}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(P) \times \operatorname{en}_{\rho\sigma}(Q) = 0. \end{equation*}
