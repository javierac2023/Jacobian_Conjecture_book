Proposition 1.4.12.

Let \(P,Q \in K[x,y]\setminus \{0\}\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} v_{\rho\sigma}([P,Q]) \le v_{\rho\sigma}(P) + v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) - (\rho+\sigma), \end{equation*}
for each \((\rho,\sigma) \in \mathfrak{V}\text{.}\) Moreover,
\begin{equation*} v_{\rho\sigma}([P,Q]) = v_{\rho\sigma}(P) + v_{\rho\sigma}(Q) - (\rho+\sigma) \iff [\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(Q)] \ne 0, \end{equation*}
and, in this case, \([\ell_{\rho\sigma}(P), \ell_{\rho\sigma}(Q)] = \ell_{\rho\sigma}([P,Q])\text{.}\)
